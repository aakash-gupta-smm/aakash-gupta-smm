How I Work

Most marketers own one channel.
I run them together.

01

Start with the business, not the channel

Who actually buys, what they search for, where they spend their time, and how long the decision takes. Channel choices come after this. Picking Google Ads or Instagram before understanding the customer is how budgets get wasted.

02

Fix the foundation first

Website or store, analytics and conversion tracking, SEO basics, and the social presence people check before they buy. Almost every business is missing at least one of these — and everything built on top of a broken foundation underperforms.

03

Build the organic engine

SEO, content and social media are the part that compounds. Search visibility, a brand voice that sounds like a person, and a community that engages. This keeps working when the ad budget pauses — which is exactly why it comes before paid.

04

Add paid to accelerate, not to replace

Google Ads and Meta Ads layered onto a foundation that already converts. Start narrow, prove it works at a small budget, scale what performs and cut what doesn't.

05

Measure the whole picture, not one channel

GA4 and Google Tag Manager for measurement, Looker Studio for reporting, Meta Business Suite for paid social. Tracking configured properly, and reporting that shows how organic, social and paid actually work together. Revenue and cost per customer — not clicks, not impressions, not follower counts.
