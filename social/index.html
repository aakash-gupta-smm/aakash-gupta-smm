How I Work

Most accounts post a lot.
Very few post on purpose.

01

Audit what is already there

Before planning anything new, I look at what has been published and what it actually did. Most accounts I take over have plenty of output and almost no strategy — high post counts, flat engagement, and an algorithm that has quietly stopped distributing them.

02

Build content pillars, not a posting schedule

A calendar full of slots is not a strategy. I define three or four pillars the brand can credibly own, then every post has to earn its place inside one of them. It makes the account recognisable and it makes planning fast.

03

Lead with the format that gets distributed

Reels carry reach right now, so they take 60–70% of output, with carousels and statics doing the jobs Reels cannot. The mix follows what the platform is actually pushing, not what is easiest to produce.

04

Fix the profile people land on

Reach is wasted if the grid, bio and highlights do not hold the visitor. A reel brings someone to the profile; the profile decides whether they follow. I treat it as a landing page, not a gallery.

05

Show up in the comments, not just the feed

Community is the part most brands skip. Replying, engaging and being present in the niche is what compounds, and it is what turns an audience into conversations the sales team can actually use.

06

Report on reach and revenue, not vanity

Monthly reporting on reach, engagement rate and follower growth — and where paid social is running, on cost per result too. The content mix changes based on what the numbers say, not on what felt good to post.
